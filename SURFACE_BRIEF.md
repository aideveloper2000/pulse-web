# Surface brief — Pulse web app

Scope: the Pulse web interface (Operate mode). Audience: Udhai and listeners he shares the app with. Job: go from intent to hearing music, then organise it — likes, playlists, uploads. Action: play, queue, save, upload. Proof: real playable catalog, real persisted library. Constraints: dark listening-room context, dense lists, one player anchor, no manufactured claims.

## Direction contract

THESIS: the interface recedes so the music leads — a dark listening room where the player is the anchor and every surface exists to put a track on in the fewest moves. It refuses the category default of a marketing hero and a wall of identical cards.

OWN-WORLD: near-black ground (#0a0a0e) with elevated surfaces in #121219/#1a1a23; one vivid accent (pulse green #1ee06f) reserved for play, save and live state; Satoshi self-hosted for all type; artwork is the only saturated colour on the page; 12–14px radii; soft offset shadows; tabular numerals for time and counts.

STORY: a listener opens Pulse, sees a greeting and six things worth playing right now, and is one click from audio. From there the shelf rows (trending, made-for-you, new releases, artists, genres) carry exploration, and the bottom player carries every session.

FIRST VIEWPORT: greeting and identity at the top, then a 3×2 quick-pick tile grid (each tile a cover, a title, one click to play), then the first content shelf. The player bar is pinned to the bottom of the viewport from the first paint, showing the empty state ("Pick something to play") rather than hiding.

FORM: pinned direction — "Create a Spotify like app" (Udhai's words). seed: none (pinned).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
